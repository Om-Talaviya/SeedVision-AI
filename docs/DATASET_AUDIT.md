## 5. Protocol for Ingesting Real Agronomic Seed Data

To transition SeedVision AI from a verified software pipeline to an empirical agricultural tool, researchers must follow this standardized protocol:

### Step 1: Physical Acquisition & Photography Setup
1. **Target Pulse:** Chickpea (*Cicer arietinum L.*), Desi and Kabuli varieties.
2. **Imaging Environment:** Neutral matte background (18% neutral gray card or matte off-white laboratory tray), diffuse uniform illumination (LED light ring, $\ge 5000\text{K}$) to eliminate harsh shadows.
3. **Capture Device:** High-resolution digital camera or industrial RGB macro lens (minimum 300 DPI, uncompressed or low-compression JPEG).
4. **Sample Count:** Recommended minimum 100–250 physical seeds per class across multiple agricultural seed lots.

### Step 2: Expert Annotation & Disagreement Logging
- Classify seeds according to AGMARK / USDA GIPSA physical defect standards:
  - `sound_healthy`: Intact seed coat, normal pigmentation, zero mechanical fractures.
  - `discolored_spotted`: Fungal lesions, necrotic spots, moisture weathering.
  - `broken_cracked`: Thresher impact cleavage, split cotyledons, fractured coat.
  - `shrivelled_damaged`: Withered cotyledons, bruchid beetle boreholes, stunted growth.
- Record annotator disagreements to measure inter-annotator agreement (Cohen's Kappa $\kappa$).

### Step 3: Automated Ingestion via CLI
Place collected images into `data/real_raw/<class_name>/` and run:
```bash
python scripts/prepare_dataset.py --data-dir data/real_raw --manifest-out data/processed/splits_real/
```
